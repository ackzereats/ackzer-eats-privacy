Ackzer Eats Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Ackzer Eats ("Ackzer Eats", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and handled when you use the Ackzer Eats mobile application.

By using Ackzer Eats, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on the features you use, Ackzer Eats may collect or process:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, phone number, user/account identifier, and authentication information.
  • Delivery information, such as delivery addresses that you provide.
  • Order information, including products ordered, quantities, prices, order status, delivery details, and order history.
  • App activity associated with features such as favourites and reviews.
  • Information required to provide account, ordering, notification, customer-support, and security functionality.
  • Technical information that may be processed automatically by service providers used by the app, such as device, diagnostic, authentication, or network-related information.

We do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary for providing or operating Ackzer Eats.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We may use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Process and manage food orders.
  • Provide delivery and saved-address functionality.
  • Display order history and order status.
  • Provide favourites, reviews, and other requested app features.
  • Send service-related notifications or order updates where enabled.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, or unauthorized access.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Ackzer Eats uses third-party technology and infrastructure to provide parts of the service. These may include Google Firebase services for functionality such as authentication, database services, and related app infrastructure, and Supabase for certain hosted media or storage functionality.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy terms and policies. Their processing may include technical information necessary to provide, secure, and maintain their services.

Ackzer Eats does not sell users' personal information.

5. PAYMENTS

Ackzer Eats may provide payment instructions or access to payment services. Where a payment is completed through a bank, payment provider, or other third-party service, that provider may process the payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Unless expressly stated in the app, Ackzer Eats does not itself store full payment-card details.

6. DATA SHARING

We may share information only where reasonably necessary to operate the service, including with:

  • Service providers that support the app's infrastructure and functionality.
  • Personnel or parties involved in fulfilling and managing an order where necessary.
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect legal rights, users, or the service.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

7. DATA RETENTION

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the service, maintaining appropriate business or transaction records, resolving disputes, security, and complying with legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

8. DATA SECURITY

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and should not share passwords with others.

9. ACCOUNT DELETION AND USER CHOICES

Where available, users may request or initiate deletion of their Ackzer Eats account through the functionality provided in the app or by contacting us.

When an account is deleted, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate, except for information that must or may reasonably be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record, or dispute-resolution purposes.
