Delete Your Ackzer Eats Account

Ackzer Eats users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by following the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to ackzereats@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete My Account.
  3. Send the request from the email address associated with your Ackzer Eats account.
  4. Clearly state that you would like your Ackzer Eats account and associated personal data deleted.

We may need to verify your request before processing the deletion to help protect your account from unauthorized requests.

Data that may be deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, personal information associated with your account may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate. This may include:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Saved delivery information.
  • Information associated with favourites and other account-specific app features.
  • Other personal information that is no longer reasonably necessary to operate the service or meet applicable requirements.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record, accounting, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate compliance purposes. This may include certain order or transaction records.

Need help?
For account deletion or privacy assistance, contact Ackzer Eats at ackzereats@gmail.com.
